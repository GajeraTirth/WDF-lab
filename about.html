About the University

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is a leading private university located in Changa, Anand, Gujarat. The university provides quality education, research opportunities, and industry-oriented learning through its various institutes and departments.

The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programs in Engineering, Computer Applications, Pharmacy, Management, Nursing, Applied Sciences, and other disciplines.

Vision

To become a world-class university by promoting excellence in education, research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and community development.

Mission

  • Provide quality education using modern teaching methods.
  • Promote research and innovation.
  • Develop skilled professionals with ethical values.
  • Encourage entrepreneurship and leadership.
  • Serve society through knowledge and technology.
